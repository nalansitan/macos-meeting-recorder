Core ML models are downloaded only on explicit user action from:
https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/speaker-diarization-coreml
The pipeline uses pyannote segmentation and WeSpeaker speaker embeddings.
Model authors and upstream terms are recorded on the model repository/model card;
format conversion does not replace upstream model licenses.
No audio, speaker embeddings, or names are uploaded for inference.
